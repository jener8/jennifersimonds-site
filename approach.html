How I work

UX beyond
the interface.

My UX work extends beyond screens into research, problem framing, stakeholder alignment, accessibility, design systems and strategic decision-making — especially in complex, regulated and public-sector environments.

What that looks like in practice

  • Research & discovery

    Identify who is affected, what they need to understand, and where trust breaks — through interviews, usability testing, journeys and co-creation.

  • Problem framing

    Separate the user problem from the solution space before committing to flows, components or AI features.

  • Stakeholder alignment

    Facilitate workshops and decision forums so product, legal, engineering and policy can move with a shared understanding of risk and value.

  • Accessibility

    Treat BITV, WCAG and platform guidelines as design inputs — shaping navigation, components, typography, contrast and interaction patterns — balanced with usability and technical feasibility, not as a late compliance checklist.

  • Design systems

    Build and govern component libraries so complex products stay coherent as teams and platforms scale.

  • Strategic decisions

    Balance user needs with technical, regulatory and organisational constraints — and make the trade-offs visible.
