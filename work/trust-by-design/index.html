Case study — Responsible AI

Trust by Design: from principles to AI guidelines

Public institutions don't fail at AI because the models are weak. They fail because staff can't see, question or correct what the system does. Trust by Design came out of embedded research with 14+ public sector AI implementation teams, and turns that gap into four testable guideline questions.

The four pillars

  • Explainability

    Can staff see why the system suggested this, in language they understand?

  • Override

    Can a person reject or correct the output, and is doing so easy and expected?

  • Recoverability

    Can errors be found and reversed without lasting harm to the person affected?

  • Accountability

    Is a named role responsible for every AI-supported decision?

From framework to guidelines

Each pillar becomes a question that a team, a works council or a supervisory body can check before a system goes live. That makes the framework usable for writing AI policy, not only for designing interfaces.
