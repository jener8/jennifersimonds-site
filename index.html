Featured UX projects

Projects where accessibility shaped the design — not as a late checklist, but as product quality balanced with usability, platform conventions and delivery constraints.

01 / Government · Native · Accessibility

i-Kfz — Digital Vehicle Registration

Citizens needed a secure way to handle digital driver’s licences and vehicle registration — under legal, authentication and multi-stakeholder constraints, and to BITV accessibility requirements for a public service.

My contributionLed UX/UI end-to-end for Germany’s first BITV-certified government app (Android & iOS). Accessibility requirements informed the native design system I owned — component behaviour, contrast, touch targets and interaction patterns — while I built the UX team from zero to six.

02 / Research · Public sector · Inclusive UX

EU Digital Driving Licence

Digital ID apps across the EU risk feeling opaque or untrustworthy — especially for older adults, people with disabilities and anyone less familiar with digital services.

My contributionLed UX research using journeys, paper prototyping, co-creation and surveys to surface trust, comprehension and access barriers — so inclusive and accessible use could inform design where regulation and real-world use meet.

03 / Human-centred AI · Inclusion

EquitAI — Responsible AI for Job Applications

Recruitment systems under-represent non-standard career paths; AI can add speed but also risk overwriting the applicant’s voice and judgement.

My contributionDesigned and built an AI agent prototype that starts from barriers faced by migrant and refugee women — defining what AI should automate, augment, or leave in human control so the experience stays usable, fair and human-led.

About

Senior UX Designer & UX Strategist.
Berlin-based. Dual citizen.

I work beyond interface design into research, problem framing, stakeholder alignment, accessibility, design systems and strategic decision-making — helping organisations ship products people can use, understand and trust.

Accessibility is a core design capability in that work: I treat BITV, WCAG and platform guidelines as inputs to interaction design — balancing them with usability, technical feasibility and user needs — not as a separate compliance exercise.

More than 10 years across government digital services and regulated technology. Most recently Senior UX/AI Design Specialist at Bundesdruckerei — built a UX team from zero to six, delivered Germany's first BITV-certified government app with 1M+ downloads, and led design ops, design systems and research strategy.

MA in Design for Responsible AI. Fluent in English and German. Available for senior UX, lead UX and UX strategy roles — and selected consulting.
