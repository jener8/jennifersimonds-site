01

Public Sector Native App Design System BITV/WCAG

i-Kfz — Digital Vehicle Registration

Problem: Citizens needed secure digital driver’s licence and vehicle registration under legal and multi-stakeholder constraints, meeting BITV requirements for a public service.
My role: Led UX/UI end-to-end for Germany’s first BITV-certified government app. Accessibility requirements informed the native Android/iOS design system I owned — component behaviour, contrast, touch targets and interaction patterns — while I built the UX team from zero to six.

Design Systems Work

Design systems aren't deliverables — they're the infrastructure that determines whether a product stays coherent as it scales. Here's how I approach them.

Native UI Systems

Android & iOS Component Libraries

Built native design systems for government apps — covering component behaviour, accessibility states, touch targets, and BITV compliance across both platforms. Used by the UX team I built from zero to six.

AI Interface Patterns

AI Interaction & Explainability Patterns

Design patterns for AI-powered interfaces — including explainability components, uncertainty indicators, human override flows, and trust-building interaction sequences for regulated environments.

Accessibility Systems

BITV/WCAG-Compliant Design Frameworks

Designing accessibility in from the foundation rather than auditing it in at the end — semantic structure, colour contrast systems, keyboard navigation patterns, and screen reader-compatible component libraries.
