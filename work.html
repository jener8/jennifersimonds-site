Research-led design for the EU's rollout of digital identity apps across member states. The challenge: making systems that feel secure and trustworthy to citizens — especially older adults and those unfamiliar with digital services. I developed a user research framework combining usability testing, co-creation, and system storytelling.

Health Tech IoT B2C

End-to-end UX design for a B2C health app connecting with a biometric IoT device to influence users' moods. The design challenge was translating invisible, complex technology into an experience that felt intuitive and emotionally resonant for non-technical users across Android and iOS.

Design Systems Work

Design systems aren't deliverables — they're the infrastructure that determines whether a product stays coherent as it scales. Here's how I approach them.

Native UI Systems

Android & iOS Component Libraries

Built native design systems for government apps — covering component behaviour, accessibility states, touch targets, and BITV compliance across both platforms. Used by a 64-person team.

AI Interface Patterns

AI Interaction & Explainability Patterns

Design patterns for AI-powered interfaces — including explainability components, uncertainty indicators, human override flows, and trust-building interaction sequences for regulated environments.

Accessibility Systems

BITV/WCAG-Compliant Design Frameworks

Designing accessibility in from the foundation rather than auditing it in at the end — semantic structure, colour contrast systems, keyboard navigation patterns, and screen reader-compatible component libraries.
